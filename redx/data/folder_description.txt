redx/data -- template spectra bundled with RedX. Checked 2026-10-14; spiff/ added 2026-10-22.
Only SUDA and SPIFF spectra are bundled: their CC BY 4.0 licence allows redistribution (and irtf_parallax.csv, a
table of published parallaxes, below). The other template sets carry no licence
that allows redistribution, so redx/sources.py downloads them from their providers on first use, verifies the
download checksum and builds the templates below into REDX_CACHE (default ~/.cache/redx).
Air or vacuum wavelengths: not stated in the Selsing and SWIRE files; at SPHEREx R ~ 40 the difference
(3e-4) is negligible.

suda/  57 files, teff<Teff>_logg<logg>.txt (Teff 700-3000 K)
  Columns: wave_um, normalized_flux, normalized_flux_err, n_contrib; one Teff/logg bin per file.
  Source: SPHEREx Ultracool Dwarf Spectrophotometric Atlas (SUDA; Tu, Wang, Huang, Chen & Liu 2026, ApJ 1008,
  250, doi:10.3847/1538-4357/ae9082), Zenodo record 20659257 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.20659257), v1, 2026-06-12,
  https://zenodo.org/records/20659257, licence CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Byte-identical to atlas/spectra/ in SUDA.zip (md5 5394c30540804f62182887e7e5612e17), unmodified.
  normalized_flux is f_lambda: the median of the atlas' uJy source spectra in each Teff bin matches
  normalized_flux * lambda^2. RedX converts it to f_nu on reading.

  resolution). Source: SPIFF SPHEREx standards of ultracool dwarfs M8-Y1 with gravity and subdwarf classes
  (Gagne et al. 2026, accepted by ApJ, arXiv:2604.22012), Zenodo record 19051216 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.19051216),
  version QR2-2026-04-24, licence CC BY 4.0, downloaded 2026-10-22: spiff_templates_hybrid_csv.zip
  (md5 5e00408a1f284059f993e9da112954fe = Zenodo checksum; sha256 b867eecf1fc12cc11f649d8e960e83d6832cc518c20baf5cba6712e1f4eefe5a),
  spiff_templates/hybrid_csv/<type>_spherex_standard.csv. Content unchanged; only the file names differ. d/sdT8

  spiff_tables/spherex_template_grids_fulltable.csv; blue_extension = the suda/ template used below 1.007 um, the one

irtf/  optional, downloaded on request (python -m redx.sources --irtf) or imported from local files
  (python -m redx.sources --irtf-from DIR; FITS files with 3 rows and an SPTYPE keyword, not *_ext.fits, copied
  unchanged). IRTF Spectral Library (Rayner, Cushing & Vacca 2009, ApJS 185, 289): every <type>_<star>.fits linked from
  https://irtfweb.ifa.hawaii.edu/~spex/IRTF_Spectral_Library/References_files/All.html, from .../Data/<file>; 230
  files listed on 2026-10-22, 228 returned (M3.5IabFe-1var?_HD14488 and G3Ib-IIWkH&Kcomp?_HD192713 give 404).
  Rows: wavelength [um], f_lambda [W m-2 um-1], error; 0.8-2.4 um, 184 reach >= 4 um. No licence stated.
  The 4 planets (plnt_*) are not used, nor 5 giants without a parallax (irtf_parallax.csv). Classification uses the 178
  of the remaining 219 that reach 4.0 um, extended to 5.2 um as f_nu ~ lambda^-2 from the median of their last 0.1 um;
  star typing uses all 219, compared only over the wavelengths they measure. f_nu in uJy as observed (f_lambda
  lambda^2 / c); a fit amplitude a puts the source at d = d_star / sqrt(a).
