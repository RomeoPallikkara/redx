redx/data -- template spectra bundled with RedX. Checked 2026-10-14.
Only SUDA is bundled: its CC BY 4.0 licence allows redistribution. The other template sets carry no licence
that allows redistribution, so redx/sources.py downloads them from their providers on first use, verifies the
download checksum and builds the templates below into REDX_CACHE (default ~/.cache/redx).
Air or vacuum wavelengths: not stated in the Selsing and SWIRE files; at SPHEREx R ~ 40 the difference
(3e-4) is negligible.

suda/  57 files, teff<Teff>_logg<logg>.txt (Teff 700-3000 K)
  Columns: wave_um, normalized_flux, normalized_flux_err, n_contrib; one Teff/logg bin per file.
  Source: SPHEREx Ultracool Dwarf Spectrophotometric Atlas (SUDA; Tu, Wang, Huang, Chen & Liu 2026, ApJ 1008,
  250, doi:10.3847/1538-4357/ae9082), Zenodo record 20659257 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.20659257), v1, 2026-06-12,
  https://zenodo.org/records/20659257, licence CC BY 4.0. Byte-identical to atlas/spectra/ in SUDA.zip
  (md5 5394c30540804f62182887e7e5612e17),
  unmodified. normalized_flux is f_lambda: the median of the atlas' uJy source spectra in each Teff bin
  matches normalized_flux * lambda^2. RedX converts it to f_nu on reading.

selsing/  selsing16_smc_ebv{000,005,010,020,030,050}.sed, rest wavelength [A] and f_lambda [arbitrary], 2200
  log-spaced points over 1000-250000 A. Built from Selsing2015.dat, the X-shooter quasar composite of Selsing et
  al. (2016, A&A 585, A87, doi:10.1051/0004-6361/201527096), https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jselsing/
  QuasarComposite/master/Selsing2015.dat (sha256 dce055acdd378444e35acad292ccf2fb00c2dc381216e5cc1d81083753f11c12;
  repository README: "Feel free to download and use, but please cite as Selsing et al. 2016 A&A 585, A87").
  Recipe: zero (masked) pixels dropped; beyond the red edge a power law f_lambda ~ lambda^-1.5 normalised to the
  mean flux over 11150-11350 A; resampled to the grid; SMC extinction of Pei (1992, ApJ 395, 130,
  doi:10.1086/171637; fit of Table 4, R_V = 2.93 of Table 2, A_B = (R_V + 1) E(B-V)) at E(B-V) = 0, 0.05,
  0.10, 0.20, 0.30, 0.50. Reproduces byte for byte the files bundled with earlier versions (checked 2026-10-14).

swire/  {Ell5,Sc,M82,Arp220}_template_norm.sed, rest wavelength [A] and f_lambda [arbitrary], from the SWIRE
  template library (Polletta et al. 2007, ApJ 663, 81, doi:10.1086/518113),
  http://www.iasf-milano.inaf.it/~polletta/templates/data/swire_library.tar.gz
  (sha256 34cce03a16fde1b3f0da0a39b84ac38ba5c1d526697200d72beed0fed3f9b48c). The library page asks users to
  cite Polletta et al. (2007). Sc and M82 contain 2 and 3 exact duplicate rows, which RedX drops on reading.
